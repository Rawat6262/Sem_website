Common questions

What do you need from me for a quote?

The event name, city, dates and your stall size are a good start. Your logo, brand guidelines and a few stalls you like will help us get closer on the first attempt. Not sure of the details yet? Send what you have and we will help with the rest.

How soon will I hear back?

Our team aims to reply with ideas and a free quote within 24 hours of receiving your enquiry.

Can I see the design before you build it?

Yes. We create 3D visuals of your stall so you can review it and ask for changes before fabrication starts.

Do you work outside Ludhiana?

Yes. We deliver stalls across India, with regional offices in Delhi, Chandigarh, Pune and Bengaluru, and we take on projects in Germany, Dubai and Turkey.

Can you handle only part of the project?

Yes. You can hire us for one service, such as 3D design, fabrication or printing, or for a complete turnkey stall.
