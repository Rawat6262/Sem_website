Things I noticed in the PDF

  • Some photos look AI-generated (stalls, workshop, people). Real photos of your own work will look more trustworthy, and clients can tell the difference.
  • Client names and reviews look like sample content (Agrimax, Technovolt, Novex, Amit Sharma…). We need real ones, or that section stays hidden until we have them.
  • No mobile design in the PDF. I'll make every section stack properly on phones.
  • No footer or contact section in the PDF. I'll design them to match the same style.
  • Some small text in the PDF is hard to read, so some wording on the site may differ slightly from the PDF.
