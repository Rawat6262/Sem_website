How a project in another city works

  1. Share the venue and dates

    Tell us the city, event and stall size, wherever you are.

  2. Review your design remotely

    See your stall in 3D and ask for changes from your own office.

  3. We build and dispatch

    Our workshop fabricates the stall and sends it to the venue.

  4. We install on site

    Our team installs the stall and supports you at the venue.

Questions about locations

Do you work in cities where you have no office?

Yes. We deliver stalls across India, and our teams travel with the stall for installation. Tell us the venue and dates, and we will confirm how we can support you there.

Can you handle a stall for an exhibition abroad?

Yes. We take on projects in Germany, Dubai and Turkey, with installation support overseas. Share the event details to get started.

How can I review the design if I am in another city?

You don't need to visit us. We create 3D visuals of your stall, so you can review it and ask for changes from wherever you are.

How early should I contact you?

As early as you can. Send your event dates, even if the details aren't final, and we will tell you what is possible.
