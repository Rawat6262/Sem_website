Creative spaces, stronger brands

A Ludhiana-based team that designs, builds and installs exhibition stalls across India and overseas.

Exhibition space that works as hard as your sales team

SEM Group designs, fabricates and installs exhibition stalls. We are headquartered in Ludhiana, and for more than 14 years we have helped brands across India and abroad turn a rented patch of floor into real business conversations.

Everything sits under one roof: design, 3D visualization, fabrication, branding and on-site installation. You deal with one team from the first sketch to the moment the show opens, so nothing is lost between a designer, a workshop and a site contractor.

Alongside stalls we manage corporate events and create interiors, using the same people and the same attention to finish.
